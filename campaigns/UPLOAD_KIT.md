## Rules that keep you paid (Reach campaigns)
- Name in the caption/text: **Clix** / **Rage**. Hashtag: **#Clix** / **#yourrage** (always included below)
- No logos, nothing negative, no reposting the same clip on one platform, no bought views/likes (one strike = banned from every Reach campaign)
- **Submit each post's link in Whop → Content Rewards within 30 minutes of posting**

## How to post so the algorithm gives it a fair shot
- **Order:** post today's top 4 first (marked ⭐), 1–2 hours apart, alternating Clix / Rage. Post the rest tomorrow. Dumping 11 at once splits your reach.
- **Best time:** 4–9 pm US Eastern (audience must be 50%+ US/UK/CA/AU/NZ). From India that's about 2:30–7:30 am IST.
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload at youtube.com/upload → paste Title + Description → "No, it's not made for kids" → Public. It's under 60 s and vertical, so it becomes a Short automatically.
- **Instagram Reels:** + → Reel → paste Caption → turn **"Also share to Feed" ON** → Share. Instagram allows max **5 hashtags**, so the captions below use 5.
- Reply to the first comments within the first hour. Pin a comment that repeats the question.
- Don't delete and re-upload a clip that starts slow. That looks like reposting.
